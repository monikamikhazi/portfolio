About

Research isn’t just reporting—it’s helping teams move wisely.

I’m a UX researcher focused on messy, high-stakes product decisions: where adoption breaks down, where confidence frays, and where teams need evidence before they scale. My work blends qualitative understanding with product strategy so the right insight lands at the right moment.

Before research, I worked across product operations, stakeholder communication, and service design, which gave me a practical lens for how organisations actually make decisions. That background helps me translate research into strategy, not just reports.
